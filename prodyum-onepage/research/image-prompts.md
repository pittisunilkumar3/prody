Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original luxury design-studio editorial still life for a website's branding and digital chapter. Wide 16:9. A large sculptural folded sheet of matte deep vermilion-red paper curves diagonally through an ivory architectural gallery, with a sphere made of softly frosted translucent glass half visible behind it. Strong interplay of paper surface and negative space, subtle printed-paper texture without any actual writing, warm directional sunlight, crisp long shadows, minimal arrangement on pale limestone. Gallery-quality art direction, photographic realism, tactile and sophisticated. Limit palette to brick red, ivory, and soft grey. The composition must be striking as a fullbleed crop. No logos, letters, text, UI, watermark, chrome, wires or screens. This is concept art representing identity and digital craft.
